Example 1 — kinetic typography beat-cut MV (T2VA, English):

Core idea (integrated_multimodal_description)
6 seconds, 9:16. Bold layered kinetic typography reading "SUMMER NEVER SLEEPS" sweeps across a midnight city rooftop. Cinematic vertical 9:16, 24 fps.

[Shot 1] Medium-wide, slight low angle. The text snaps onto the beat — one phrase per beat, letters sliding in with overlap, kerning tightening as they land. Letters are bold condensed sans, neon white with magenta-blue glow edges. The camera handheld-pushes in with small amplitude, synced to the beat. The rooftop is empty; deep purple-black sky, distant city bokeh. End on the word "SLEEPS" frozen alone.

Soundscape (overall_soundscape)
Distant city ambience, faint wind across the rooftop, a soft metallic tick on each letter landing.

Music (non_diegetic_music)
Punchy electronic beat at ~120 BPM, sub-bass swell on the final freeze, bright synth stabs on each word snap.

Do not include
No people, no logos, no extra text beyond "SUMMER NEVER SLEEPS".

Example 2 — environmental T2VA (no character, English):

Core idea (integrated_multimodal_description)
8 seconds, 16:9. A single raindrop hits a still neon puddle at night and sends out perfect concentric ripples. Live-action cinematic, macro.

[Shot 1] Extreme close-up, top-down on the puddle. Neon teal and magenta reflections shimmer on the black water surface. The camera holds dead still. The raindrop strikes center frame at ~00:01.500; ripples expand outward in perfect rings, distorting the neon reflection. A second drop strikes at ~00:04.000 offset left. Shallow depth of field; bokeh of distant streetlights breathes slightly.

Soundscape (overall_soundscape)
A single clear "plink" on each impact, faint hiss of light rain, distant traffic hum.
